Rules (simple)

Har trade ka data ledger mein public hai

Entry: Top 500 — 🚀 Pump/Breakout = LONG, 🩸 Dump/Breakdown = SHORT. Alag tag: ↩ Mean-reversion — 🧊 Oversold = LONG, 🔥 Overbought = SHORT. Sirf jab signal naya shuru ho (ledger start par pehle se chal rahe signals par trade nahi). Wohi signal 6 ghante ke andar wapas aaye to duplicate trade nahi. Ek coin par ek waqt mein ek hi trade.

Size: $100, qty = 100 / entry price. Entry price = signal milte hi fetch ki gayi live quote (CoinGecko, warna Binance). 20 min se purani quote kabhi use nahi hoti.

Exit (jo pehle ho): ulta signal · take-profit · stop-loss · 7 din. Trend LONG: TP = sell zone (resistance), warna +2 ATR; SL = −1.5 ATR. SHORT ulta (TP = buy zone/support). Mean-reversion: TP 1.5 ATR, SL 1.5 ATR. ATR distance ki hadd: SL max 15%, TP max 25%. Candles/ATR na hon: TP 12%, SL 8%.

Void: agar baad mein data-quality check se signal hi invalid nikle (jaise pegged/wrapped ya low-volatility token, ya source ki price 20 min se purani thi), trade "VOID" band hoti hai aur stats mein shamil nahi hoti — record phir bhi dikhta hai.

Check: har ~15 min box par live price se. Exit price = us check ki price, is liye TP/SL level se thori behtar ya buri ho sakti hai. App mein open trades ka P&L har 60 s live price se.
