Market: CoinGecko top 500 (rank, price, supply, ATH) har ~10 min. App khuli ho to prices har 60 s CoinGecko se live; CoinGecko rate-limit (429) par Binance fallback, aur 10 min se purani price "⏱ time" ke saath purani dikhti hai — live nahi.

Trend: sirf closed candles (aaj ki adhuri candle shamil nahi) — daily EMA20/50/200 — price > EMA50 aur EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 = Uptrend, ulta = Downtrend, warna Range. 4h trend aur RSI14 bhi. Candles: Binance → Bybit → OKX → Gate → MEXC (USDT pair, CoinGecko price se 5% ke andar match hona zaroori — warna pair reject), warna CoinGecko daily closes. 12h se purane candles ya 8%+ price farq ho to zones hide.

Zones: pichle 180 din ke swing high/low (pivot) levels, 0.5×ATR ke andar cluster. Buy zone = nearest support ±0.25 ATR; Sell zone = nearest resistance ±0.25 ATR (levels price se kam az kam 0.5 ATR door). Distance % live price se.

Signal: 🩸 Dump/Breakdown = support toota ya 24h girawat (≥ max(6%, 1.5×ATR)) volume ke saath · 🚀 Pump/Breakout = resistance ke upar ya utni hi tezi · 🔥 Overbought RSI ≥ 75 (4h ≥ 80) · 🧊 Oversold RSI ≤ 28 (4h ≤ 20) · 👀 X Hype = X mentions normal se 3x+ (ya 24h mein 2x) lekin price flat. Volume spike = pichle 24h (6 closed 4h candles) volume / us se pehle ke 7 closed din ka average. ⚪ Low volatility = daily ATR < 0.3% (fund/NAV tokens) — RSI/pump/dump label nahi lagta.

X hype: har ghante ~20 coins ki X post counts (48h hourly) rotation mein: rank 1–30 har ~4h, 31–100 har ~12h, baqi ~3 din, aur volume-spike coins har ~3h. Spike = abhi ki rate / normal (24h average + kal isi waqt).

Report: CoinGecko (project, links, supply, listings), CoinPaprika (team), DefiLlama (TVL, fees, revenue). Unlock schedule ka free source nahi mila — is liye "data nahi mila" likha hota hai.

Financial advice nahi hai. Zones aur signals sirf data par mabni andaze hain — DYOR, stop-loss aur position size khud tay karein.
